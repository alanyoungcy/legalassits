# Capco employment practice profile

> Draft for attorney review — not legal advice. Jurisdiction rules must be researched per matter.

## Practice

- Organization: Capco (a Wipro company)
- Primary regions: Hong Kong, United Kingdom
- Role: employment legal assist for hiring, exits, and classification screening

## Matter workspaces

- Enabled: ✗

## Hiring review triggers

- Offer letter with restrictive covenants
- Cross-border hire (HK ↔ UK / other)
- Senior / client-facing roles with non-solicit
- Contractor vs employee classification uncertain

## Restrictive covenant policy

- Prefer narrow non-solicit over broad non-compete
- Always run jurisdiction-specific enforceability check at review time
- Flag missing consideration / garden leave / notice interactions

## Shared guardrails

- Destination check before sharing investigation or termination memos
- Do not state black-letter law from memory — research and cite for the hire jurisdiction
